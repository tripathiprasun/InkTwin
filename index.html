Teach it your handwriting.

Print the calibration sheet, fill in the boxes with your own pen, then upload a photo or scan. It covers every key on a normal keyboard.

  1. Download the calibration sheet and print it on A4. Don't crop it: all four black squares must stay visible.
  2. Each group has a big character on the left. A small word under it (like “low L”, “zero”, “quote”) tells look-alikes apart.
  3. Write that character once in each of its three boxes, the way you naturally write it. Small differences between the three are good.
  4. Sit letters on the dashed line. Letters like g j p q y and symbols like , ; ( ) may dip below it. Keep tall letters inside the box (about 7 mm).
  5. Use a dark pen at normal pressure. If you slip, leave that box empty rather than scribbling over it.
  6. Photograph the page straight from above in even light with the whole page in frame (or scan at 150–300 dpi), then upload it.

Copy these lines onto any plain paper with your own pen, then upload one photo.

Loading the lines to copy…

  1. Copy each line exactly, in this order, one line per row, with an empty row between lines.
  2. Write in separate letters, like neat print. Joined letters can't be told apart.
  3. Leave a clear gap between words. Use your normal size and a dark pen.
  4. Photograph the page from straight above in even light, with the page filling the frame.
